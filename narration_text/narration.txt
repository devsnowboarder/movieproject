Our journey begins with a walk across the expansive,
stone-paved pathways of the Hiroshima Peace Memorial Park,
moving toward the iconic architecture of the memorial buildings
in the distance.
As we continue, we arrive at the Children's Peace Monument,
which is beautifully surrounded by glass booths filled
with thousands of vibrant,
hand-folded origami paper cranes.
Visitors gather around the tall memorial statue to pay
their respects and document the colorful
displays of hope left by people from all over the world.
 Moving further into the park, we encounter the Flame of
 Peace and the Cenotaph for the A-Bomb Victims,
 featuring a striking arched concrete monument set over a
  tranquil pool of water.
